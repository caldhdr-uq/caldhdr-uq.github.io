About the forum

A safe, respectful and collaborative community

The Culturally and Linguistically Diverse (CALD) HDR Engagement Forum is a welcoming and inclusive space for Higher Degree by Research students from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds at The University of Queensland.

The forum is a safe, respectful and collaborative environment that sustains the wellbeing and academic success of CALD HDR students. It connects candidates, advisors and staff across every UQ faculty and institute.

Whether you are starting your candidature, midway through, or preparing to submit, the forum offers peer support, practical skills development and a community that understands the CALD HDR experience.
